AI Tournament

Ninaro's five computer players play each other: AI Random just picks a column, and each one up adds a rule: Novice takes a win, Normal blocks the other player's win, Expert won't drop where the other player can then win on top, and Genius also weighs how much room each column leaves to win, and (new in this version) won't let the other player set up two wins at once. Every pairing plays the same number of games, taking turns to go first, using the 1998 game's random numbers.
